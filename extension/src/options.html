How we estimate

AI companies don't publish per-message energy, so these are careful estimates. We show a likely range of 0.4× to 3× around each figure.

  1. Tokens. In English about 4 characters make one token. Each reply is charged for the conversation before it, because chat apps re-read it every time.
  2. Energy per token depends on model size, in Wh per 1,000 tokens read and written:
    SizeExamplesReadWritten
    SmallGemini Flash, GPT mini, Claude Haiku0.010.10
    MediumGPT-5, GPT-4o, Claude Sonnet, Gemini Pro0.050.50
    LargeClaude Opus0.121.50
    Extra largeDeep research, Claude Fable0.253.00
    A typical medium-model reply comes to about 0.3 Wh, in line with what Google (0.24 Wh per Gemini prompt) and OpenAI (0.34 Wh per ChatGPT query) published in 2025.
  3. Thinking models write hidden reasoning first, so their output counts 4×. Generated images add about 2.9 Wh each.
  4. Prompt cache. When you reply in a chat within 5 minutes, the provider still has the conversation cached, and re-reading it takes about a tenth of the compute.
  5. Other languages. Hindi, Arabic, Russian, Chinese and other scripts use more tokens per character than English, and we count them that way.
  6. Carbon is energy × the grid intensity you choose above. Water is energy × 1.1 L/kWh of data-centre cooling.

Not included: training the models, building the hardware, and your own device. Sources and code are public; corrections are welcome.
