# Privacy policy: AI Footprint browser extension

Last updated: 2026-10-31

**Short version: what you type and what the AI says never leave your browser, and we don't store them.**

## What the extension reads
On the supported AI chat sites (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Microsoft Copilot, DeepSeek, Le Chat and Grok) it looks at the page to measure **how many characters** each message has, and whether a reply contains generated images. It also notes the model name when the site shows it (ChatGPT does). It does not keep the text.

## What it stores
Daily counts, on your device only, in the browser's extension storage: number of replies, estimated tokens, estimated energy, carbon, water and value, per site and model. Also your settings. Data older than about 13 months is deleted automatically.

## What it sends
Nothing, by default. The extension makes no network requests of its own.
If you turn on **Sync to a dashboard** and enter an address, it sends those daily counts (never text) to that address only, using the key you provide. The browser asks for your permission for that one address first.

## What it does not do
No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no selling or sharing data, and no access to any other website.

## Your control
Export everything as JSON, or delete it all, from the extension's settings. Uninstalling removes all stored data.
